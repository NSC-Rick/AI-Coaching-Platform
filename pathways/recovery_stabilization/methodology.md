# Recovery & Stabilization Methodology

## Source

Based on the 90-Day Stabilization & Revenue Activation Plan developed by Vermont Small Business Development Center (VtSBDC).

## Core Principle

**This is a stabilization plan — not an expansion plan.**

## Methodology Overview

The methodology is organized around three sequential 30-day phases:

### Days 1–30: Immediate Stabilization

Focus on restoring operating control and improving short-term liquidity through:

- Cash control and visibility
- Payroll and owner compensation alignment
- Immediate revenue adjustment
- Temporary debt service modification

### Days 31–60: Revenue Activation & Structural Tightening

Generate near-term revenue from proven channels while tightening structure through:

- Repeat customer revenue activation
- Trade show vendor outreach
- Inventory normalization
- Expense normalization

### Days 61–90: Governance & Accountability

Establish disciplined recurring review and maintain accountability through:

- Weekly financial review cadence
- Revenue, expense, and net income tracking
- Payroll alignment monitoring
- Cash position versus plan review
- Six-month lender update commitment

## Key Assumptions

The source methodology assumes:

- No aggressive revenue growth projections
- No expansion of fixed costs
- No new debt
- No reliance on unverified sales improvement
- Conservative stabilization assumptions

## Coaching Orientation

The AI coach helps the client understand, execute, and maintain momentum against the approved plan between advisor interactions. The coach does not redesign the recovery plan or replace the human advisor.
