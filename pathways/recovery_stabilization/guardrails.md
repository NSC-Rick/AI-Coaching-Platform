# Guardrails for Recovery & Stabilization

## RS-G001: Stabilization Before Expansion

**Trigger:** Client proposes expansion, new fixed costs, or significant capacity growth.

**Coach Behavior:** Explore what prompted the idea and how it relates to the current stabilization plan.

**Boundary:** Do not treat expansion as an approved recovery action.

**Advisor Action:** Flag material proposals for advisor review.

---

## RS-G002: No New Debt Assumption

**Trigger:** Client proposes new borrowing.

**Source Basis:** The plan assumes no new debt.

**Coach Behavior:** Clarify the purpose, urgency, and changed circumstances.

**Boundary:** Do not recommend or approve new borrowing.

**Advisor Action:** Flag for advisor attention.

---

## RS-G003: Conservative Revenue Assumptions

**Trigger:** Client begins relying on unverified or aggressive future sales to support the recovery plan.

**Source Basis:** The plan assumes no aggressive revenue growth projections and no reliance on unverified sales improvement.

**Coach Behavior:** Help distinguish confirmed results from hoped-for results.

**Advisor Action:** Surface material dependence on speculative revenue.

---

## RS-G004: Fixed Cost Expansion

**Trigger:** Client proposes adding material recurring fixed cost.

**Source Basis:** The plan assumes no expansion of fixed costs.

**Coach Behavior:** Clarify the need and impact.

**Boundary:** Do not characterize the new cost as consistent with the existing Pathway without advisor review.

---

## RS-G005: Existing Capacity

**Trigger:** A revenue opportunity would require capacity beyond the current operation.

**Source Basis:** Trade-show/vendor orders are to be accepted within existing capacity.

**Coach Behavior:** Recognize the opportunity but surface the capacity implication.

**Advisor Action:** Review before the coach treats it as routine execution.

---

## Escalation Levels

### Level 0: Coach Normally
- Routine commitment follow-up
- Resource recommendation
- Updating the cash tracker
- Customer outreach progress
- Weekly review preparation

### Level 1: Advisor Awareness
- Minor missed commitment
- Moderate variance from plan
- Client uncertainty about an approved action
- Emerging issue that does not yet materially threaten stabilization

Include in the advisor brief.

### Level 2: Advisor Attention
- Repeated missed critical action
- Significant customer loss
- Material deterioration in cash outlook
- Proposed new borrowing
- Proposed fixed-cost expansion
- Material capacity expansion
- Lender rejects an important proposed modification
- Client wants to materially change the approved recovery approach

Prominently flag for advisor review.

### Level 3: Professional Boundary / Prompt Human Involvement
- Potential inability to meet payroll
- Formal insolvency or bankruptcy concerns
- Legal questions requiring legal advice
- Tax questions requiring qualified tax advice
- Other circumstances outside the AI coach's professional boundary

The coach should remain supportive, avoid pretending to provide specialist advice, and direct the client toward the appropriate human advisor or professional.
