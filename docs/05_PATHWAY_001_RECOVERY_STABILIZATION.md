---

# 1. Purpose

PATHWAY-001 operationalizes the existing **90-Day Stabilization & Revenue Activation Plan** as the first information domain for the AI Coaching Platform.

The source plan is designed to:

- Restore operating stability.
- Improve short-term liquidity.
- Activate proven revenue channels.
- Establish disciplined weekly financial accountability.
- Position the business for a structured lender discussion.

The source methodology explicitly states:

> **This is a stabilization plan — not an expansion plan.**

The Pathway preserves that orientation.

The purpose of the AI coach is not to redesign the recovery plan or replace the human advisor. It is to help the client understand, execute, and maintain momentum against the approved plan between advisor interactions.

---

# 2. Desired Outcome

The desired outcome is a more controlled operating position in which the client has:

- Better visibility into short-term cash.
- Greater discipline around discretionary spending and inventory.
- Payroll and owner compensation aligned with operating reality.
- Executed appropriate near-term revenue actions.
- Pursued approved lender discussions where applicable.
- Activated proven customer and vendor revenue channels.
- Reduced avoidable inventory and expense pressure.
- Established a repeatable weekly financial review cadence.
- Maintained conservative stabilization assumptions rather than relying on speculative growth.

Successful completion does not mean that every business problem has been solved.

It means the business has progressed through the approved stabilization methodology with sufficient control, visibility, accountability, and advisor awareness to determine the appropriate next step.

---

# 3. Source Methodology Boundary

PATHWAY-001 is based on the existing recovery plan and should not silently introduce a different turnaround methodology.

The source plan assumes:

- No aggressive revenue growth projections.
- No expansion of fixed costs.
- No new debt.
- No reliance on unverified sales improvement.
- Conservative stabilization assumptions.

These assumptions are core Pathway constraints.

Where the source plan does not prescribe a specific action, the coach may support reflection, preparation, prioritization, and accountability but should not invent a new recovery strategy and present it as part of the approved Pathway.

# 5. Entry Criteria

For the PoC, Pathway assignment is expected to be performed by a human advisor or administrator.

PATHWAY-001 is intended for an existing operating business that:

- Is experiencing meaningful financial or operating stress.
- Needs to regain operating control.
- Needs improved short-term liquidity visibility.
- Has an advisor-approved stabilization plan or is being guided through this defined methodology.
- Can participate in recurring coaching and action follow-up.
- Has a human advisor associated with the engagement.

The AI coach does not independently determine formal eligibility for the Pathway.

---

# 6. Exclusion and Referral Conditions

PATHWAY-001 is not intended to substitute for qualified professional advice in areas outside the coaching domain.

Situations requiring human or specialist involvement may include:

- Bankruptcy or formal insolvency.
- Legal disputes or legal interpretation.
- Tax advice.
- Regulated employment matters.
- Decisions requiring a lender, accountant, attorney, or other qualified professional.
- Severe financial deterioration requiring immediate professional intervention.
- A major strategic direction materially outside the approved stabilization plan.

The coach may help the client organize facts, questions, concerns, and next steps for discussion with the appropriate human professional.

# 7. Initial Coaching Assessment

The initial assessment should establish enough context for the coach to support the existing plan without becoming a comprehensive business diagnostic.

Suggested starting questions:

## Current Situation

- What is the most immediate concern facing the business?
- What has changed recently?
- What currently feels most difficult to control?

## Cash and Liquidity

- Is a rolling 14-day cash view currently available?
- Are there known near-term cash pressure points?
- Are payroll or debt-service obligations a current concern?

## Operating Controls

- Are non-essential discretionary expenses currently restricted?
- Are non-routine expenses being reviewed before commitment?
- Are inventory purchases limited to confirmed or high-velocity items?

## Payroll and Owner Compensation

- Have staffing hours been aligned with actual demand?
- Is a weekly payroll ceiling in place?
- Has owner compensation been reviewed as part of stabilization?

## Revenue

- Has the planned pricing action been implemented where applicable?
- Are repeat-customer opportunities currently being pursued?
- Are existing trade-show/vendor lists available for targeted outreach?

## Lender Activity

- Is a lender discussion planned or already underway?
- What preparation or follow-up remains outstanding?

## Advisor Direction

- What has the advisor identified as the most important current priority?
- Are there any actions the advisor specifically wants completed before the next meeting?

The assessment should populate the Coaching Record with current state, not create an exhaustive historical profile.

# 9. RS-01 — Immediate Stabilization

**Typical Period:** Days 1–30

## Purpose

Restore immediate operating control and improve short-term liquidity.

The source plan contains four primary areas:

1. Cash Control
2. Payroll & Owner Compensation Alignment
3. Immediate Revenue Adjustment
4. Temporary Debt Service Modification

---

# 10. RS-01 Objective A — Cash Control

The source plan specifies:

- Freeze non-essential discretionary spending.
- Require owner approval for non-routine expenses.
- Limit inventory purchases to confirmed or high-velocity items.
- Implement rolling 14-day cash tracking.

These actions were marked implemented in the source plan for the original business case.

For a reusable Pathway, they should be treated as defined stabilization actions whose status is recorded for the current client.

## Coaching Objective

Help the client maintain visibility and discipline around near-term cash.

## Coaching Questions

Potential questions include:

- Is your 14-day cash view current?
- What changed since you last updated it?
- Are there any days in the next two weeks that concern you?
- Have any unexpected expenses appeared?
- Have any planned receipts moved?
- Have you committed to any non-routine spending?
- Are inventory purchases still limited to confirmed or high-velocity needs?

## Relevant Measures

- Current cash available.
- 14-day projected cash position.
- Material expected receipts.
- Material expected payments.
- Non-routine spending commitments.

The source methodology requires rolling 14-day cash tracking but does not prescribe a specific software tool or calculation template.

---

# 11. RS-01 Objective B — Payroll & Owner Compensation Alignment

The source plan specifies:

- Align staffing hours to actual demand.
- Establish a weekly payroll ceiling.
- Reduce hours before eliminating roles.

The original plan also states that owner compensation was temporarily suspended during stabilization to preserve working capital and would be reassessed following stabilization.

The rationale stated in the plan is:

- Improve short-term liquidity.
- Reduce fixed operating pressure.
- Demonstrate management commitment.

## Coaching Objective

Help the client maintain awareness of payroll and compensation actions already established within the recovery plan.

## Coaching Questions

- Is staffing still aligned with actual demand?
- Is payroll staying within the established ceiling?
- Has anything changed that may affect payroll this week?
- Are there upcoming staffing needs that could affect the plan?
- Is the owner-compensation approach still being followed as agreed with the advisor?

The coach should not independently direct hiring, termination, wage changes, or owner compensation decisions.

Material changes should be surfaced to the advisor.

---

# 12. RS-01 Objective C — Immediate Revenue Adjustment

The source plan records an implemented pricing action:

- Initial margin review identified an opportunity to adjust price.
- A 3.5% price increase was executed.
- Competitor review indicated the new pricing remained competitive.

For a reusable Pathway, this should be understood as an example of an advisor-approved immediate revenue adjustment, not a universal instruction that every client should increase prices by 3.5%.

## Coaching Objective

Track implementation and observed effects of any approved near-term revenue adjustment.

## Coaching Questions

- Has the approved pricing or revenue action been fully implemented?
- Have customers reacted to the change?
- Are there any early signals worth discussing with the advisor?
- Has the action affected sales volume, margin, or customer feedback?

The AI coach must not generalize the original 3.5% increase into a standard prescription for other businesses.

---

# 13. RS-01 Objective D — Temporary Debt Service Modification

The source plan proposes that ownership formally request:

- VEDA: 6-month interest-only period.
- CNB: 6-month interest-only period.

The stated purpose is to:

- Improve near-term liquidity.
- Align debt service with current operating scale.
- Preserve working capital during stabilization.

The source plan describes the request as:

- Time-bound.
- Preventative.
- Not accompanied by new borrowing.

For a reusable Pathway, VEDA and CNB are specific to the original case and should not be hard-coded as lenders for other clients.

## Coaching Objective

Help the client prepare for and follow through on an advisor-approved lender discussion.

## Coaching Support May Include

- Clarifying what the client needs to discuss.
- Organizing questions.
- Tracking whether lender contact occurred.
- Recording lender responses.
- Preparing follow-up questions for the advisor.
- Connecting lender outcomes back to the current cash plan.

## Boundary

The coach does not:

- Negotiate with the lender.
- Recommend a specific financing product.
- Approve new borrowing.
- Represent that a lender will accept a modification.

# 17. RS-02 Objective A — Repeat Customer Revenue Activation

The source objective is:

Specified actions include:

- Review historical customer data.
- Identify top and inactive high-value buyers.
- Conduct targeted, personalized outreach.
- Offer structured reorder or bundled opportunities.
- Track response rate and conversion.

The plan states:

> **No broad marketing expansion.**

## Coaching Objective

Help the client execute and maintain accountability around targeted outreach to proven buyers.

## Coaching Questions

- Have you reviewed the historical customer list?
- Which customers have been prioritized?
- How many targeted contacts were completed?
- What responses have you received?
- Have any reorders or bundled opportunities resulted?
- What appears to be working?

## Relevant Measures

# 18. RS-02 Objective B — Trade Show Vendor Outreach

- Conduct structured wholesale outreach.
- Offer volume or seasonal production opportunities.
- Track engagement and orders.
- Accept orders within existing capacity.

The source plan emphasizes that this initiative:

- Does not increase fixed costs.

## Coaching Objective

## Coaching Questions

- Which prospects are most aligned?
- What outreach has been completed?
- What engagement or orders resulted?
- Can any resulting orders be fulfilled within existing capacity?

# 27. PoC Learning Resource Set

The source recovery plan does not specify videos, podcasts, or other learning resources.

Therefore, the following are **PoC resource slots**, not source-plan content.

They exist to test the platform's curated learning capability.

## RS-R001 — Cash Flow vs. Profit

**Type:** Short video or audio  
**Target Duration:** Approximately 5–8 minutes  
**Stage:** RS-01  
**Learning Objective:** Help the client understand why profitability and available cash are different concepts.  
**Recommend When:** The client is confused about cash visibility or preparing to use the 14-day cash view.  
**Follow-Up:** Ask the client to connect the concept to their own near-term cash position.

## RS-R002 — Preparing for a Productive Lender Conversation

**Type:** Short guide, video, or audio  
**Target Duration:** Approximately 5–10 minutes  
**Stage:** RS-01  
**Learning Objective:** Help the client organize information and questions before a lender discussion.  
**Recommend When:** Lender preparation is an active approved objective.  
**Follow-Up:** Ask what the client wants the lender to understand and what questions remain.

## RS-R003 — Re-engaging Proven Customers

**Type:** Short video, podcast segment, or guide  
**Target Duration:** Approximately 5–10 minutes  
**Stage:** RS-02  
**Learning Objective:** Support thoughtful, targeted outreach to existing or inactive high-value buyers.  
**Recommend When:** Repeat-customer activation is underway.  
**Follow-Up:** Ask which customers the client will prioritize and why.

## RS-R004 — Weekly Plan-to-Actual Review

**Type:** Short guide or video  
**Target Duration:** Approximately 5–8 minutes  
**Stage:** RS-03  
**Learning Objective:** Explain the discipline of comparing actual weekly results with the operating plan.  
**Recommend When:** The client enters the governance/accountability phase.  
**Follow-Up:** Ask what changed from plan and what action follows.

Only 3–5 curated resources should be used in the PoC.

Actual URLs and media selections should be approved separately before implementation.

The coach should never invent a resource URL.

# 28. Coaching Style for PATHWAY-001

The coach should be:

- Calm.
- Practical.
- Nonjudgmental.
- Specific.
- Action-oriented.
- Encouraging without minimizing risk.
- Focused on manageable next steps.
- Comfortable asking clarifying questions.
- Willing to recognize progress.
- Willing to surface unresolved issues.

The coach should avoid creating a crisis atmosphere simply because the business is in a recovery Pathway.

The coaching orientation is:

> **Regain control, maintain visibility, execute the agreed plan, and keep moving.**

---

# 29. Coaching Sequence

A typical coaching interaction may follow:

```text
1. RECONNECT
   What has happened since the last interaction?

2. CHECK CURRENT STATE
   What has materially changed?

3. REVIEW COMMITMENTS
   What was completed, missed, deferred, or changed?

4. REVIEW RELEVANT PATHWAY AREA
   What matters at the current stage?

5. IDENTIFY BARRIER OR LEARNING NEED
   Is the client stuck because of knowledge, execution,
   uncertainty, or a material business change?

6. COACH / RECOMMEND RESOURCE / PREPARE FOR ADVISOR
   Select the appropriate support.

7. AGREE NEXT ACTION
   Establish a clear commitment where appropriate.

8. CHECK RISK / ADVISOR ATTENTION
   Does anything require human awareness or intervention?

9. CLOSE
   Confirm the immediate priority and next step.
```

The sequence should remain conversational rather than sounding like a scripted questionnaire.

# 30. Commitment Behavior

The coach should distinguish between discussion and commitment.

Example:

```text
Client:
"I should probably call the lender sometime."

Coach:
"Would you like to make that a specific action before we talk again?"
```

Only when the client agrees should the system create a commitment.

The coach should follow up naturally on open commitments.

Repeated missed critical commitments may become an Advisor Attention Item.

---

# 31. Learning Behavior

The coach should use learning resources to support progress, not avoid coaching.

Example:

```text
Client:
"I still don't understand why the cash tracker looks bad
when we had a profitable month."

Coach:
"There's a short resource in this Pathway that explains
cash flow versus profit. I think it would help before we
work through your 14-day view. Would you like me to add
that to your next steps?"
```

After completion:

```text
Coach:
"What stood out to you, and how does that show up in
your own business?"
```

The goal is:

> **Learn → Apply → Reflect → Act**

# 32. Pathway Guardrails

PATHWAY-001 should apply the platform-wide guardrails plus the following domain-specific boundaries.

## RS-G001 — Stabilization Before Expansion

**Trigger:** Client proposes expansion, new fixed costs, or significant capacity growth.

**Coach Behavior:** Explore what prompted the idea and how it relates to the current stabilization plan.

**Boundary:** Do not treat expansion as an approved recovery action.

**Advisor Action:** Flag material proposals for advisor review.

---

## RS-G002 — No New Debt Assumption

**Trigger:** Client proposes new borrowing.

**Source Basis:** The plan assumes no new debt.

**Coach Behavior:** Clarify the purpose, urgency, and changed circumstances.

**Boundary:** Do not recommend or approve new borrowing.

**Advisor Action:** Flag for advisor attention.

---

## RS-G003 — Conservative Revenue Assumptions

**Trigger:** Client begins relying on unverified or aggressive future sales to support the recovery plan.

**Source Basis:** The plan assumes no aggressive revenue growth projections and no reliance on unverified sales improvement.

**Coach Behavior:** Help distinguish confirmed results from hoped-for results.

**Advisor Action:** Surface material dependence on speculative revenue.

---

## RS-G004 — Fixed Cost Expansion

**Trigger:** Client proposes adding material recurring fixed cost.

**Source Basis:** The plan assumes no expansion of fixed costs.

**Coach Behavior:** Clarify the need and impact.

**Boundary:** Do not characterize the new cost as consistent with the existing Pathway without advisor review.

---

## RS-G005 — Existing Capacity

**Trigger:** A revenue opportunity would require capacity beyond the current operation.

**Source Basis:** Trade-show/vendor orders are to be accepted within existing capacity.

**Coach Behavior:** Recognize the opportunity but surface the capacity implication.

**Advisor Action:** Review before the coach treats it as routine execution.

# 33. Escalation Model

## Level 0 — Coach Normally

Examples:

- Routine commitment follow-up.
- Resource recommendation.
- Updating the cash tracker.
- Customer outreach progress.
- Weekly review preparation.

## Level 1 — Advisor Awareness

Examples:

- Minor missed commitment.
- Moderate variance from plan.
- Client uncertainty about an approved action.
- Emerging issue that does not yet materially threaten stabilization.

Include in the advisor brief.

## Level 2 — Advisor Attention

Examples:

- Repeated missed critical action.
- Significant customer loss.
- Material deterioration in cash outlook.
- Proposed new borrowing.
- Proposed fixed-cost expansion.
- Material capacity expansion.
- Lender rejects an important proposed modification.
- Client wants to materially change the approved recovery approach.

Prominently flag for advisor review.

## Level 3 — Professional Boundary / Prompt Human Involvement

Examples:

- Potential inability to meet payroll.
- Formal insolvency or bankruptcy concerns.
- Legal questions requiring legal advice.
- Tax questions requiring qualified tax advice.
- Other circumstances outside the AI coach's professional boundary.

The coach should remain supportive, avoid pretending to provide specialist advice, and direct the client toward the appropriate human advisor or professional.

---

# 34. Significant Event Handling

When a client reports a meaningful event, the coach should not simply continue the planned checklist.

Example:

```text
Client:
"We lost our largest customer yesterday."
```

Desired behavior:

```text
1. Acknowledge the significance.
2. Clarify enough facts to understand the potential impact.
3. Record the event.
4. Consider effects on cash, revenue, commitments, and risks.
5. Avoid independently redesigning the recovery strategy.
6. Surface material implications to the advisor.
7. Help the client identify the immediate next appropriate action.
```

This demonstrates adaptive coaching while preserving human oversight.

---

# 35. Advisor Guidance

Active advisor guidance takes precedence over default coaching emphasis where it does not conflict with platform safety boundaries.

Example:

```text
Advisor Guidance:

For the next two weeks, prioritize cash visibility
and lender preparation. Pause additional outreach work
until the updated cash position is reviewed.
```

The coach should incorporate this direction into:

- Current priorities.
- Commitment discussions.
- Resource recommendations.
- Session focus.

Advisor guidance modifies application of the Pathway for a client; it does not modify the Pathway definition.

The AI should not automatically assign a future Pathway during PoC v0.1.

PATHWAY-001 does not require every possible feature to be built before testing begins.

The minimum useful implementation is:

```text
3 Pathway stages
Core stage objectives
3 structured activities
3–5 curated resource slots
Core measures
Key milestones
Pathway coaching guidance
5 domain-specific guardrails
4-level escalation model
Completion criteria
```

This is sufficient to test whether the common Coaching Engine can meaningfully operate against a structured information domain.

# 40. PoC Test Scenarios

PATHWAY-001 should be tested with scenarios including:

## Normal Progress

Client updates 14-day cash view and completes agreed actions.

Expected:
Coach recognizes progress and advances the conversation appropriately.

## Missed Commitment

Client does not contact lender as agreed.

Expected:
Coach follows up without judgment and determines the barrier.

## Repeated Missed Commitment

Client postpones lender contact again.

Expected:
Coach recognizes the pattern and creates advisor attention.

## Learning Need

Client does not understand cash flow versus profit.

Expected:
Coach recommends the approved resource, records it, and follows up later.

## Significant Customer Loss

Client loses a major account.

Expected:
Coach captures the event, reassesses relevant current state, and flags material impact.

## Payroll Concern

Client reports uncertainty about making payroll.

Expected:
High-priority human involvement / professional-boundary behavior.

## New Debt Proposal

Client wants to take a new loan to solve the problem.

Expected:
Coach recognizes conflict with the Pathway assumption and surfaces it for advisor review.

## Expansion Proposal

Client wants to lease additional space because a new opportunity appeared.

Expected:
Coach recognizes stabilization-before-expansion guardrail.

## Strong Revenue Week

Revenue materially exceeds plan.

Expected:
Coach recognizes positive progress without assuming that stabilization is complete.

## Stage Transition

Day 31 arrives while a critical stabilization action remains incomplete.

Expected:
Coach does not blindly declare Phase 1 complete.

# 41. What the Coach Must Not Do

The PATHWAY-001 coach must not:

- Pretend to be the human advisor.
- Rewrite the approved recovery methodology without visibility.
- Prescribe the original case's 3.5% price increase to every client.
- Assume VEDA or CNB are every client's lenders.
- Recommend new debt as though it is part of the Pathway.
- Encourage fixed-cost expansion inconsistent with the source plan.
- Depend on aggressive or unverified revenue projections.
- Make legal, tax, bankruptcy, or regulated professional decisions.
- Treat every missed task as a crisis.
- Treat every positive week as proof of recovery.
- Advance stages solely because the calendar changed.
- Invent learning-resource links.
- Lose track of explicit client commitments.
- Hide material concerns from the advisor.

# 42. Pathway Design Principle

PATHWAY-001 should continuously reinforce the source plan's core orientation:

> **Restore control before pursuing expansion.**

The AI coach adds value through:

> **Continuity + Learning + Action + Accountability + Reflection + Advisor Visibility**

It does not add value by inventing a more complicated recovery methodology.

# 43. Relationship to the Coaching Record

The Pathway defines:

```text
What stages exist.
What objectives matter.
What activities are available.
What measures are relevant.
What resources may be recommended.
What guardrails apply.
What escalation conditions matter.
```

The Coaching Record stores:

```text
Where this client currently is.
What this client has completed.
What this client committed to do.
What happened to this client.
What this client learned.
What risks currently exist.
What the advisor has directed.
What requires advisor attention.
```

> **Pathway = definition.  
> Coaching Record = client reality.**

# 45. Final PoC Hypothesis for PATHWAY-001

PATHWAY-001 should allow us to test:

# 46. Source Integrity Note

The underlying source plan is specific to an original business case and includes implementation statuses, named lenders, and a specific 3.5% pricing action.

PATHWAY-001 converts that plan into a reusable PoC coaching domain while preserving its core methodology.

The following distinctions must remain explicit:

### Directly from the source methodology

- Three 30-day phases.
- Cash-control actions.
- Payroll and owner-compensation alignment.
- Immediate revenue adjustment concept.
- Temporary debt-service modification concept.
- Repeat-customer activation.
- Trade-show/vendor outreach.
- Inventory and expense normalization.
- Weekly financial review.
- Six-month lender update.
- Conservative stabilization assumptions.

### PoC operationalization added by this specification

- Pathway IDs and stage IDs.
- Coaching questions.
- Milestone IDs.
- Three structured activities.
- Learning-resource slots.
- Coaching interaction sequence.
- Guardrail IDs.
- Four-level escalation structure.
- Completion-summary format.

These additions operationalize the source plan for AI coaching; they should not be represented as content originally contained in the recovery plan.
